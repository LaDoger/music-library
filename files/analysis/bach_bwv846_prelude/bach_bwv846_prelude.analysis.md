# Johann Sebastian Bach: Well-Tempered Clavier: Prelude in C major

Source: `files/scores/bach_bwv846_prelude.mid`, absolute quarters [0, 140.0]; 4/4, C, opening ♩≈60.0. MIDI is referenced, not copied.

A single arpeggio figure, one chord per bar, carries the whole prelude. It moves from a C-major frame toward G (tonicized, not modulated), returns to C at bar 19, darkens through chromatic diminished sevenths, holds an eight-bar dominant pedal, and ends over a tonic pedal.

## Story and key journey

- Bars 1–4: **Opening frame**. I–ii42–V65–I over a C bass. Mood: calm, clear.
- Bars 5–11: **Toward G**. Applied chords tonicize G; arrival on G at bar 11. Mood: open, hopeful.
- Bars 12–19: **Return to C**. Diminished sevenths and a falling bass lead to C at bar 19. Mood: reflective, steady.
- Bars 20–23: **Chromatic turn**. C7, Fmaj7 and two diminished sevenths over a chromatic bass. Mood: darkening, searching.
- Bars 24–31: **Dominant pedal**. Eight bars on G; cadential 6/4 and sus chords delay resolution. Mood: suspended, tense.
- Bars 32–35: **Coda**. Tonic pedal; the figure breaks up and closes on C. Mood: settled, serene.

## Tricks checked against the notes

- Bars 1–4: I–ii42–V65–I over a held bass. The voice-leading moves by step and the bass hardly moves.
- Bars 6–11: applied V42/V and V7/V make G feel like home briefly without a real key change.
- Bars 20–23: bass F–F#–G#–G under diminished sevenths builds tension into the pedal.
- Bars 24–31: dominant pedal with cadential 6/4 and V7sus4 chords, repeated to stretch the wait for the tonic.
